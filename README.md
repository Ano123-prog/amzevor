AMZEVOR

Connect. Grow. Do Business.

AMZEVOR is an upcoming global business social networking platform designed to bring individuals, entrepreneurs, small businesses and companies together.

Our goal is to make it easier for people and businesses to connect, promote products and services, discover opportunities and grow their networks.

Our Mission

To make business connections, visibility and opportunities more accessible to people and businesses everywhere.

Our Vision

To become a trusted global platform where individuals and businesses can connect, collaborate and grow together.

Planned Features

1. Personal and business profiles.
2. Social networking and business updates.
3. Posts, likes, comments and sharing.
4. Business discovery and search.
5. Product and service listings.
6. Business connections and followers.
7. Notifications.
8. Business promotion opportunities.

Technology

The initial website prototype uses:

1. HTML
2. CSS
3. JavaScript

Additional technologies will be introduced as the platform develops.

Development Status

AMZEVOR is currently in its early development stage. The current version is a demonstration prototype. Features such as user registration, shared posts, database storage and business accounts are planned for future development.

Our Goal

We want to build a platform where businesses of all sizes can gain visibility, connect with customers and discover new opportunities without unnecessary barriers.
